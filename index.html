05 · How I Work
How I want to show up at work

I have real respect for the people I work with — not as a talking point, it's just how I operate. I'll sit in a conversation I know nothing about and still walk out having learned something, because I'm comfortable being the person who asks the question everyone else has moved past asking.

I'm not shrewd, and I've stopped pretending I need to be. If you're looking for someone who gets to efficiency by playing the political game, that's not me.

My way there is slower, and I think more durable: understand what the people around me are actually dealing with — inside the work and outside it — and build enough mutual respect and transparency that the relationship doesn't have to get renegotiated every time something gets hard.
