Case study 03 · 0→1 product

Launching supplier monetization from zero

ScopeWorker's marketplace had no monetization layer on the supplier side. I owned it from concept to a live product with 850+ active suppliers.

My role
PM — concept through launch, solo
Starting point
0 suppliers, no product
Result
850+ active suppliers
Context

A two-sided marketplace with only one side monetized

ScopeWorker's platform connects mobile buyers with suppliers across a live, running marketplace. The buyer side was monetized; the supplier side wasn't — there was no product to build on, and no existing revenue mechanic to extend. It had to be built from nothing, on top of a platform that couldn't be disrupted for existing users.

What I did

Owned it end to end, as the only PM on it

I took this from concept to launch myself — as one of the products I run solo, since I'm the only PM across two of ScopeWorker's three squads. That means the scoping, the go-to-market mechanics inside the product, and the iteration after launch were all on me, in parallel with the rest of what I own.

Result

Zero to 850+ active suppliers

0 suppliers → 850+ active

The product is live and adopted at 850+ active suppliers today. This is one of the two 0→1 monetization and platform builds I point to when the question is whether I can take something from nothing to shipped, not just optimize what already exists.
